## Why we're starting fresh

The old website can't be edited any more — the login for it is gone, and there's no way to get it back.

The good news is that **you own the domain name, getstrength.com**, and that's the part that really matters. It means when the new site is ready, we can put it at your normal web address. Visitors won't notice anything except a better site.

Your gym email address will keep working. We'll take care of that during the switch-over — it's the one thing we'll be careful about.

## What the new website will have

Five pages, in this order:

| Page | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Who you are, and a clear way to join the gym |
| **Blog** | Your training articles, newest first |
| **The Gym** | Onehunga location, 24/7 access, membership options, map |
| **About Us** | Your history, your coaches, who you've trained |
| **Contact** | Address, email, and a form people can fill in |

Plus clearly-marked links out to your other things:

- **Merch Store** (Teespring)
- **Front Squat Harness** (Amazon)
- **GS Videos** (YouTube)
- **Photos** (Facebook)

**One important change:** on the old site, the gym was the *fourth* thing in the menu, behind the shop. Someone in Onehunga looking for a gym found a blog and a shop first. On the new site, the gym comes first.

---

## What we are *not* building

We are not building a shop, a checkout, a member login, or anything that takes payments.

Those already work in your own systems, and we're not touching them. The website will simply have a button that sends people there.

This is deliberate, and it's good news for you:

- It's much cheaper and quicker to build.
- The site can't lose anyone's card details or member information, because it never holds any.
- Nothing that currently works can break.

---

## Built so it's easy to look after

Nobody could work out how the old site was put together or where to log in — that's why it was lost. This one will be different:

- **Everything is in your name.** The hosting, the domain and every login belong to GetStrength. We're just given access.
- **One page listing every login**, kept by you — not in someone's head.
- **A simple how-to guide** with screenshots, for posting a blog article or changing prices.
- **Built with common, standard tools**, so any web developer could pick it up later — not just us.
- **Nothing to update or patch.** The old site needed regular updates to stay safe. This one doesn't.

## How we'll work together

We work in **two-week cycles**, so you see the site as it's being built and can steer it — you won't be handed a finished website you've never seen.

- **You get a preview link in the first week.** It's a private copy of the site that only people with the link can see. Check it any time, on your phone, and watch it come together.
- **A progress meeting every two weeks** — about 30 minutes, in person or online. We show you what's done, you tell us what to change. Roughly week 2, week 4, and a final review before launch.
- **Your feedback is included.** Wording, photos, colours, layout, the order of things — any changes within what this document describes are part of the $1,500, with no limit on how many times.
- **New things are not included.** If you ask for something this document doesn't cover — an online booking system, a members' area, an extra section — we'll tell you the price first and you decide. We will never do extra work and surprise you with a bill.

## After the site is live

- **We'll still be here.** Small things — a typo, swapping a photo, changing prices — just ask.
- **We'll keep an eye on the site for a full year.** If anything we built breaks in that time, we fix it free. We also get an automatic alert if the site ever goes offline, so usually we'll know before you do.
- **Anything new means a fresh quote.** Adding features changes what's being built, so the price below would be recalculated and agreed with you before any work starts — never after.
- **You're never stuck with us.** Everything is in your name, so you can take it to anyone.
