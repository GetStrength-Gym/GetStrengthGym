# How to get changes made to your website

Your website doesn't have a login or an editor, so nobody at the gym needs to learn one.
When you want something changed, send us an email and we'll make the change for you.

Please put what you want in the subject line, for example "New blog post" or "Price change".

## What to send us

### A new blog post
- The **title** of the post.
- The **text** of the post. A Word document, a Google Doc or the body of the email is fine.
- A **one-sentence summary**. This appears in the list of posts and in Google results.
- The **date** you want it to go up. If you don't mind, we'll use the day we publish it.
- Any **photos** you want with it, as attachments. Please make sure you're allowed to use
  them, and that the people in them are happy to appear on the website.

### A price change
- **Which membership** the price is for, using the name it has on the website.
- The **new price**, and whether it is per week, per month, or something else.
- The **date** the new price starts.

We won't put any prices on the website until you've confirmed them with us in writing.

### Swapping a photo
- **Which photo** you want replaced. A screenshot, or a description of where it is on the
  page, is fine.
- The **new photo**, as an attachment, in the best quality you have.
- Confirmation that you're allowed to use it, and that the people in it are happy to appear.

## What happens next

1. We reply to let you know we've got your request.
2. We make the change and check it on phones and computers.
3. We put the change live on your website.
4. We email you to say it's done, with a link so you can see it.

If anything in your request is unclear, we'll ask you before making the change rather than
guess.
